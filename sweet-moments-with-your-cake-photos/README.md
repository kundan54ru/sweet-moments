# Sweet Moments — Landing Page

A fast, mobile-first HTML/CSS/JS landing page for Google Ads campaigns targeting cake orders around Tetulmari/Dhanbad.

## Files
- `index.html` — semantic page structure, SEO metadata and editable business details
- `style.css` — responsive premium visual system
- `script.js` — year, lightweight reveal animation and CTA tracking hooks

## Cake photos
The 8 photos supplied in `cake.zip` are included in `images/` and wired into the hero, cake categories and gallery. All 8 supplied photos are used; the birthday cake is reused in the hero and Birthday Cakes card.

## Google Business Profile / Maps
The current "Get Directions" buttons use a Google Maps search URL for the supplied address. Replace it with the exact Google Business Profile / Maps place URL once available.

## Policies
Privacy Policy and Terms / Refund Policy are placeholders because no policy text was supplied. Add the final policy pages before using paid traffic if required for the business.

## Google Ads / Analytics
`script.js` pushes `phone_click` and `whatsapp_click` events to `window.dataLayer`. Connect this to Google Tag Manager / GA4 conversion tracking when the tracking IDs are available.

## Important
No fake reviews, fake offers, rankings, or unsupported claims are included.
